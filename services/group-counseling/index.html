$150 per participant per group

Group topics include but are not limited to:

  • Celebrating Neurodiversity - A group devoted to exploring alternative, non-pathologizing and strengths-based frameworks for understanding what the mainstream medical system labels as “disorders” including but not limited to ADHD, Autism Spectrum Disorder, Bipolar Disorder, and Schizophrenia Spectrum Disorders. This group focuses heavily on deconstructing the language of the DSM-5, and offering more humanistic ways to understand and make meaning from participants’ lived experiences.
  • Men’s Mental Health - A closed container for individuals who identify as men, by individuals who identify as men. In this group we will explore the mental health challenges and societal stressors that are unique to the male experience, as well as deconstructing and holding space for the less visible ways that men are also oppressed and harmed within the patriarchal gender paradigm.
  • Surviving and Thriving with ADHD and Autism - This group will provide a solution focused think tank for members with similar lived experiences and support needs to share what areas of life they struggle with, and to explore what has worked for them or what they might like to try in order to live within a neurotypical world.
  • Surviving and Thriving with Complex/Chronic Illness - This group explores the existential impact of chronic illness and/or chronic pain. Participants will gain new insights and perspectives from discussions of how living with chronic pain and chronic illness impacts quality of life, and explore frameworks for finding meaning within these challenging and overwhelming experiences.
