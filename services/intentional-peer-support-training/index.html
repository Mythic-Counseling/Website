Trainings and certification in Intentional Peer Support are available by request. Submit an interest form for the training by selecting the form that reflects your involvement in Peer Support work, either as an individual looking to be certified or as an administrator of a Respite Program or other mental health organization looking to schedule an organizational training for your team to become IPS certified.
