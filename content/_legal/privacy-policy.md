This policy explains what information this website collects, why, who processes it, and the choices and rights you have. It covers the website at this address only. Information you share as a client in counseling is part of your clinical record and is governed separately (see [Health information](#health-information)).

## Who we are

This website is operated by Mythic Counseling of Florida ("we", "us"). For any question or request about your information, email [evan@mythic-counseling.com](mailto:evan@mythic-counseling.com).

## What we collect, and why

**When you browse.** This site has no advertising or social-media trackers, and its fonts and images are served from the site itself. Like every web host, our host, GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), receives technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure. We also count visits with **Cloudflare Web Analytics**, which uses no cookies, doesn't track you across sites or over time, and gives us only totals: how many visits each page gets, which site referred them, and broad device and country information. We don't receive or use any of this to identify you.

**When you contact us.** The contact form asks for your name, email address and a message, and optionally how you heard about us. It is provided by Google Forms on our Google Workspace account; if the form isn't available, it opens your own email program instead. We use this information only to reply and, if you wish, to arrange an introductory call.

**When you book an appointment.** Online booking is provided by Google Calendar appointment schedules (and, while we transition, Acuity Scheduling). It collects your name, email address and the time you choose, which we use to schedule and confirm the appointment.

**When you subscribe to the newsletter.** We collect your email address, and record when and how you subscribed, through our email newsletter service. We use it only to send the newsletter and any free resource you requested. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

**When you email us directly.** We receive whatever you include in the email.

Please don't include details about your health, symptoms or history in website forms or emails. We'll talk about what brings you to counseling in a private conversation.

## Cookies and similar technologies

This site sets no cookies of its own. It stores one small item in your browser's local storage: your answer to the cookie notice, so we don't ask again. This is strictly necessary and contains nothing about you.

The contact form sends what you type to Google Forms only when you press Send, without cookies. The booking calendar is provided by Google too, and when it loads, Google may set cookies and process your IP address under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). For that reason it (and any Google form shown embedded in a page) loads only after you agree, either in the cookie notice or when you open it. You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page, and you can always book or reach the form in a separate tab, or email us, instead.

## Legal bases (for visitors in the EU, EEA and UK)

- **Consent:** the newsletter, and loading Google's booking calendar and forms. You can withdraw consent at any time.
- **Steps before entering into a contract:** booking and confirming an appointment.
- **Legitimate interests:** replying to inquiries, keeping the website secure, and counting visits in aggregate to improve the site.

## Who processes your information

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. It is processed on our behalf only by the service providers named above: Google (Workspace, Forms and Calendar), GitHub (hosting), Cloudflare (domain services and visit counts), Acuity Scheduling (booking, during the transition) and our newsletter service. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

These providers are based in, or process data in, the United States. Where information about visitors in the EU, EEA or UK is transferred there, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

## How long we keep it

- **Inquiries and booking details** from people who don't become clients: up to two years after our last contact, then deleted.
- **Newsletter:** until you unsubscribe. After that, we keep only the record needed to make sure you aren't emailed again.
- **Hosting logs:** held by GitHub under its own retention policy.

If you become a client, your information becomes part of your clinical record and is kept as Florida law and professional standards require.

## Health information

This website is not part of your clinical record. If you become a client, the information you share in counseling is protected health information, handled under our Notice of Privacy Practices, which you receive at the start of services and which is available on request.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- access the personal information we hold about you, and get a copy;
- have it corrected or deleted;
- restrict or object to how we use it;
- receive it in a portable format;
- withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

To use any of these rights, email [evan@mythic-counseling.com](mailto:evan@mythic-counseling.com). We'll respond within one month. If you are in the EU, EEA or UK and are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children

This website is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16 through it. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change, and the date at the top will show the latest version.
