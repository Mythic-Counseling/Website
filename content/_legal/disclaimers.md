## In a crisis

This website is not a crisis service, and its forms and email inbox are not monitored for emergencies. **If you are thinking about suicide or are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, available 24/7 in the U.S.), or call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room.**

## Education and information only

The blog posts, newsletter, free resources and every other page on this site are offered for education and reflection. They are general, not tailored to you, and they are not counseling, therapy, diagnosis or medical advice. They are not a substitute for working with a qualified professional who knows your circumstances. Please don't start, stop or change any treatment or medication based on something you read here; talk with your own healthcare providers.

## No counselor–client relationship

Using the site, reading or replying to a newsletter, or contacting us does not make you a client. A counseling relationship begins only after we have both agreed to work together and completed intake.

## Licensure and location

It is not a statement of fact about any person, condition or treatment, and it may not apply to you. People respond differently to every approach, and no particular outcome is promised.
