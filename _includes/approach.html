A Person-Centered, Non-Pathologizing Alternative to Mental Health.

My approach to counseling brings together evidence-based therapeutic practices with a humanistic and existential understanding of personal growth and well-being. I believe therapy should be about more than simply alleviating distress or finding relief from life's difficulties; it should also create space for discovering who you are, understanding what matters to you, and building a life that feels meaningful and fulfilling.

Our work together is guided by your individual experiences, values, and aspirations. Rather than relying on a one-size-fits-all approach, I strive to create a therapeutic experience that honors your unique perspective, supports your personal growth, and helps you move toward the life you genuinely want to live.

My goal is to create a welcoming, comfortable, and confidential space where you feel heard, respected, and supported. From our first conversation through every step of the counseling process, I strive to provide a thoughtful, intentional experience centered around your individual needs, your personal growth, and the goals that matter most to you.
