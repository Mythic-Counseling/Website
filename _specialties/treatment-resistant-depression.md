As someone who struggled for more than a decade with treatment resistant depression, I am intimately aware of the existential implications of surviving in a place like that for long periods of time.

<!--more-->

After a while, it can become difficult to remember the person you were before, or to imagine a world in which you could become someone else or find your way back. When you spend enough time in a depressed state, eventually those depressive feelings start to become stable personality structures, and the depression becomes enmeshed and entangled with your sense of self. My therapeutic style focuses on helping clients to find meaning within the story they are living, to deconstruct the ways in which depression has begun to influence their self-concept, to explore other ways of defining themselves and the role that they are playing within their story, and to make meaningful changes towards the life that they want for themselves.
