I was diagnosed with ADHD when I was seven years old, and spent the majority of my major developmental milestones from the ages of 7-25 on some form of stimulant based psycho-pharmaceutical in order to survive the public school system as a neuro-diverse student with unmet support needs.

<!--more-->

I deeply understand the distress and trauma that comes from being a neuro-diverse individual trying to survive in a neurotypical world, and my therapeutic style when working with neuro-diverse clients reflects this. I work with clients to help deconstruct the shame, guilt, and self-blame that comes from a lifetime of being told that there’s something wrong with the way you think, process and behave because it doesn’t fit the neurotypical mold.

Whether you are a student who is really struggling against burnout in order to be successful academically while leveraging your talents without the support you need, or an adult who is struggling to navigate the complexities of modern living as a result of thinking and processing differently than the population that our system was designed for, I am confident that I can meet you where you are at in your journey of defining a sustainable and satisfying life for yourself within a world that often times feel very hostile to people who think and experience the world differently.
